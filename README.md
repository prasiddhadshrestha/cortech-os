## Conceptual Overview
CortechOS is a satirical multimedia project examining the dark intersection of subscription capitalism, neural interfaces, and the artificial inequality gap.

Grounded in Cory Doctorow's critical framework in *Tied to the Tracks: What Is Science Fiction For?*, the project refuses the techno-utopian trap of treating technology as an inevitability. Instead, it demonstrates how business models, recurring subscription fees, intellectual property barriers, and digital rights management (DRM) can turn the human mind into a proprietary, rented commodity.

### The Two Narratives
1. **Ryan K. (Platinum Member, $1,499/mo):** Working 120 hours consecutively, zero sleep, grief impulses chemically suppressed in milliseconds to maintain workplace efficiency, alienated from basic human empathy.
2. **Michael T. (Standard Member, $0/mo):** Subjected to unskippable intrusive audio ads every ten minutes inside his visual cortex, afternoon cognitive throttling, unable to afford the $5,000 cancellation fee required to retrieve his biological brain.

## Project Components
* **Interactive Web Platform:** Single-page application with pricing matrix, real-time memory quality downgrades (4K HDR vs 360p Ad-Tier), neural plan switchers, and an inescapable multi-tier cancellation flow.
* **Commercial Infomercial:** High-energy 90-second mock TV broadcast featuring the Host, customer testimonials, and an ultra-fast pharmaceutical-style legal disclaimer.
